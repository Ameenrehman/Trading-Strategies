==============================================================================
G6. IS IT A DISGUISED SMALL-CAP BET?
==============================================================================
Turnover with a negative sign is the strongest raw factor in this data,
and the one most exposed to survivorship: this universe is today's index
membership applied to history. Measured on cohorts, the effect is ~50%
stronger among symbols that entered the panel late - the names where the
bias is worst. So the score is ranked WITHIN turnover bands, and the
comparison below shows what that costs and what it buys.

  reversal, size-neutral       +8.7 bps   t   0.94   <- what is traded
  reversal, raw               +13.5 bps   t   0.39
  size alone (-turnover)       -0.9 bps   t  -1.52   <- excluded on purpose
  -> FAIL

==============================================================================
PORTFOLIO - A ROLLING BOOK OF ACTUAL POSITIONS
==============================================================================
5 slots, held up to 10 days, a freed slot refilled the
next session. With barriers off nothing frees early, so the book becomes a
10-day rebalance cycle: a fresh list of 5 names every 10 sessions.
Gross of costs.

Two design choices dominate everything else here: when you buy, and
whether you enforce a stop at all. Both are varied below against the same
picks on the same days, so the differences are the rules and nothing else.

The two rows that differ only by entry timing are the same picks on the
same days. The gap between them is the overnight move, forfeited by
waiting for the open and kept by the close-to-close benchmark.


Every stop setting loses to no stop, and the wide ones make the DRAWDOWN
worse too. That is not a paradox: stopping out of a mean-reversion trade
realises the loss the position existed to recover, and the freed slot buys
the next falling name. The SL/TP levels stay in the output as risk and
sizing context - they are not an exit rule here.

REJECTED OUT OF SAMPLE. The screener cleared all six criteria on 13
years of development data and did not replicate on the sealed 24
months. The standard error above shows this window could have called
the development-sized edge significant, so this is a real failure to
replicate rather than a window too short to tell.

What did survive: the screener still beat 20 of 20 random seeds, and
the bottom of the ranking was still the worst bucket. The sign of the
effect is intact; its size is not tradeable. That is consistent with a
weak real signal whose in-sample estimate was inflated by having chosen
the design - horizon, components, pick count - on that same window.

The holdout is now spent. Re-running this after changing the config
does not produce a second out-of-sample test, only a worse one.
